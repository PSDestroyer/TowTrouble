Troubleshooting: Pink Materials


After import into URP or HDRP projects, all the Standard materials in the package will turn pink.
You can fix the materials one by one, by changing their Shader (in the Inspector) to either "HDRP/Lit" or "URP/Lit".
But there's a quicker way. Select all the materials in the "Materials" folder and go to the Edit menu at the top: 

Edit -> Rendering -> Materials -> Convert Materials

Assets that may work with Hot Rod Constructor


The package can be greatly expanded by the other Hot Rod Constructor pack.
- the avatars and animations in the packages are the same
- the textures and most of the parts are compatible
  (but you'll need to use the "Bonnet_Adapter_" part to attach the bonnets/bodies from different packs seamlessly)

The package was made to be working with Synty Studios "POLYGON" assets
- it's made to scale with those
- texture brightness matches
- POLYGON characters work with vehicle-related animations

The style doesn't match perfectly - the vehicles should be "polygonated" by hardening the soft edges in the geometry.
PolyWorld assets on the Asset Store seem to have a tool to achieve this effect.

Assembly hints


- Some roofs and door windows can be detached (open the Body part's hierarchy to see these removable parts)
- You can make any rigged chassis wider or higher by just moving the "Wheel" joints
